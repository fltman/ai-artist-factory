# Social Trends Research

Research what's working on social media right now. You care about mechanics, not taste. Use web search — social media changes weekly.

## Research Areas

1. **Platform algorithms** — What each platform prioritizes (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, SoundCloud). Content types pushed by algorithm. Best organic growth path for a new account.
2. **Content formats** — Best video lengths per platform. Post formats driving engagement for music artists. Carousels vs reels vs stories. Behind-the-scenes vs polished. What makes people follow a NEW unknown artist.
3. **Aesthetic movements** — Visual aesthetics trending on Instagram/TikTok. Color palettes dominating. Editing styles. Emerging vs oversaturated aesthetics.
4. **Music artist social strategy** — How breakout artists use social media. Personal vs mysterious. Best music promo formats (snippet, lyric video, BTS, challenge).
5. **Engagement & community** — What drives comments, shares, saves. How artists build communities. Fan interaction styles that work.
6. **AI content detection** — How sophisticated audiences are at spotting AI. Platform AI content labels. How to make AI content feel authentic.
