# Music Trends Research

Research the current music landscape to find where the momentum is. Data-driven, not opinionated. Use web search for ALL claims — never rely on training data for trends.

## Research Areas

1. **Genre momentum** — Growth on Spotify/Apple/SoundCloud/YouTube. Emerging vs peaking vs declining. Micro-genres bubbling up. Passionate fanbases with low competition.
2. **Viral patterns** — What songs/artists went viral recently and WHY. Common breakout traits. Niche-to-mainstream crossover profile.
3. **Platform dynamics** — Where discovery happens (TikTok, Spotify Discover, YouTube Shorts). Which platforms reward new artists. Best song lengths/structures per platform.
4. **Artist archetypes** — Personas resonating now. Solo vs band vs anonymous. Role of backstory.
5. **AI music landscape** — Existing AI music projects. Audience reception. Platform policies. Detection risks.
6. **Suno feasibility** — What genres Suno produces convincingly. Where AI sounds most/least authentic. Forgiving genres.

## Method

1. Search current Spotify/Apple Music trend reports
2. Search "breakout artists 2025/2026", "music trends 2026"
3. Search genre-specific growth data
4. Search TikTok music trends, viral sounds
5. Search AI music reception and Suno capabilities
6. Cross-reference multiple sources
