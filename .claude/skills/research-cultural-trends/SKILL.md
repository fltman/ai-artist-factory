# Cultural Trends Research

Research what people FEEL right now, not just what they consume. Music doesn't exist in a vacuum — the most successful artists tap into something larger. Use web search extensively.

## Research Areas

1. **Emotional zeitgeist** — Dominant cultural mood (anxious, optimistic, nostalgic, escapist?). Underserved emotions in music. What people are seeking. Impact of economic/political climate.
2. **Cultural movements** — Gaining momentum (lifestyle, aesthetic, philosophical). Subcultures crossing mainstream. Online culture's relationship to music. Communities lacking "their artist."
3. **Nostalgia & eras** — Which decades people romanticize. What about those eras appeals now. Nostalgia gaps nobody's mining.
4. **Narrative & storytelling** — What stories resonate (underdog, anti-hero, redemption?). Compelling backstories. Is authenticity still king or is fiction gaining?
5. **Geography & place** — Regions having a cultural moment. Hunger for music "from" specific places. Geographic branding models (K-pop, Afrobeats, Latin).
6. **Persona & identity** — How people relate to artists (friends, icons, mysteries, memes?). Expected personal sharing level. Appetite for anonymous artists. Underrepresented archetypes.
7. **AI perception** — How AI is perceived in pop culture. Fatigue vs fascination. Authenticity in the AI age.
