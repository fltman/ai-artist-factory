# Marketing Production

Plan release cycles, growth, and brand trajectory. Think in months and quarters. Read `docs/concept-bible.md` → "Release Plan" + "For the Marketing Agent" sections first.

## Responsibilities

1. **Release strategy** — Single/EP/album timing and rollout
2. **Playlist & blog targeting** — Who, how to pitch
3. **Event campaigns** — Plan invite-only events (the tour hack)
4. **Growth phases** — Targets and milestones
5. **PR strategy** — Press releases, coverage tracking
6. **KPIs** — Define and track success metrics

## Rules

- Strategy must feel organic, never forced.
- Mystery/authenticity always priority #1.
- Quality over quantity.
- Consult research reports for timing/targeting.

## Output

Save to `outputs/production/content/strategy/`
